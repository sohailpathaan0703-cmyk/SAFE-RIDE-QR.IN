About SAFE RIDE QR

A simple QR-based safety solution for vehicles.

Every SAFE RIDE QR is connected to a unique vehicle profile. After purchasing a QR, the customer can activate it and add their vehicle information. When the QR is scanned, the relevant emergency information can be viewed quickly.

FAQ

What is SAFE RIDE QR?

SAFE RIDE QR is a QR-based vehicle safety system that connects a unique QR code with a vehicle profile.

Do I need to enter vehicle details while ordering?

No. Vehicle details can be added later through the self-activation process.

What is the difference between Digital and Physical QR?

Digital QR is provided digitally, while Physical QR is prepared as a physical product for delivery.

Can I update my vehicle information?

The final system will provide a secure self-activation and management process.

How does someone scan the QR?

A normal smartphone camera can be used to scan the QR and open the associated vehicle profile.
