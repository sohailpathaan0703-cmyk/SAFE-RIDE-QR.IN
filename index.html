FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is SAFE RIDE QR?

SAFE RIDE QR is a QR-based vehicle safety system connected to a unique vehicle profile.

Do I enter vehicle details while ordering?

No. Vehicle details can be added later through the self-activation process.

What is Digital QR?

Digital QR is provided digitally so the customer can use or print the QR design.

What is Physical QR?

Physical QR is prepared as a physical product and delivered to the customer's address.

How does someone scan the QR?

A normal smartphone camera can scan the QR and open the associated vehicle profile.
